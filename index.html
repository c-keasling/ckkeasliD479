Taniti is a small, tropical island in the Pacific with sandy and rocky beaches, a safe harbor, lush tropical rainforests, and a mountainous interior that includes a small, active volcano. With an indigenous population of about 20,000 and a growing tourism industry, Taniti offers an authentic island experience for every type of traveler.

Lodging

Taniti has a wide variety of lodging that ranges from an inexpensive hostel to one large, four-star resort. There are many small, family-owned hotels and a growing number of bed and breakfasts. All types of lodging are strictly regulated and regularly inspected by the Tanitian government.

Four-Star Resort

Large, full-service resort with beach access.

Family-Owned Hotels

Many small, family-owned hotels with local hospitality.

Bed & Breakfasts

A growing number of cozy bed and breakfasts.

Hostel

Inexpensive hostel for budget travelers.

All lodging is government-regulated and regularly inspected.

Transportation

Getting to Taniti

  • By Air: Small airport accommodates small jets and propeller planes. Expansion underway for larger jets.
  • By Sea: A small cruise ship docks in Yellow Leaf Bay one night per week.

Getting Around

  • Public Buses: Serve Taniti City, 5:00 a.m. to 11:00 p.m. daily.
  • Private Buses: Serve the rest of the island.
  • Taxis: Available in Taniti City.
  • Rental Cars: Available near the airport.
  • Bikes: Available to rent. Helmets required by law.
Taniti City is flat and walkable. Merriton Landing is easy to explore on foot.

Travel Tips & FAQ

Drinking Age

The drinking age is 18 and is not strictly enforced. Alcohol cannot be served or sold between midnight and 9:00 a.m.

Currency

Taniti uses the U.S. dollar. Many businesses accept euros and yen. Banks offer currency exchange. Major credit cards accepted.

Power

120 volts - same as the United States.

Language

Many younger Tanitians speak fluent English. Limited English in rural areas.

Health & Safety

One hospital with multilingual staff and several clinics. Violent crime is very rare. Some pickpocketing reported as tourism grows.

Holidays

Many national holidays. Attractions and restaurants may be closed. Plan ahead.

Best Time to Visit

Taniti enjoys warm tropical weather year-round. The dry season, May through October, is the most popular time for tourists. Be aware of national holidays when planning, as many businesses close.
